Investigation operations

Turn incident analysis into accountable follow-through.

Maven connects the initiating narrative, evidence, missing elements, findings, risk, recommended actions, assigned work, readiness, and closure.

Implemented workflow

Analysis becomes operational work.

A recommendation can become an assigned task. Missing statements and timelines stay visible. Closure remains blocked until required follow-up is resolved.

Establish evidenceRetain statements, facts, chronology, and source context.

CloseResolve blockers, record rationale, retain history.
A record that can be retraced

Evidence should survive the handoff.

The investigation workspace preserves what was reported, what was gathered, what remains missing, which actions followed, and why a determination was reached.
