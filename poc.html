Maven can assist with language. The workflow still requires evidence validation, responsible ownership, review, and an explicit closure boundary.

Evidence before language

The citation is more than an F-tag.

Resident-specific facts, equipment failures, process breakdowns, clinical findings, and source-document context shape the corrective response. Maven preserves that chain before drafting begins.

Narrative recoveredEvidence reviewedResponse controlled
Why context mattersF246 is not “assess resident needs.”The source may contain a resident, wheelchair failure, pain, activity withdrawal, and depression. Those facts belong in the operational response.
