Reference Center

Official source orientation

Source review: Links and descriptions checked October 26, 2026. Official materials can change; confirm the current source and applicable state requirements before acting.

  • Appendix PP and CMS Nursing Home Guidance CMS's nursing home guidance page links Appendix PP, survey resources, and Long-Term Care Survey Process materials used for interpretive guidance.
  • CMS Nursing Home Enforcement and Chapter 7 CMS enforcement resources, including State Operations Manual Chapter 7 and material used to assess remedies in relation to deficiency scope and severity.
  • 42 CFR Part 483 The federal regulatory requirements for long-term care facilities, including resident rights, quality of care, QAPI, infection control, and administration.
  • 42 CFR § 483.12 — Freedom from Abuse, Neglect, and Exploitation Federal requirements for reporting and investigating covered alleged violations, including the two-hour, twenty-four-hour, and five-working-day timeframes in paragraph (c).
  • CMS Exhibit 358 — Sample Facility-Reported Incident Form CMS sample initial-report fields and instructions for facility-reported incidents; state forms and submission systems may differ.
  • 42 CFR § 483.75 — Quality Assurance and Performance Improvement Federal nursing-facility requirements for a comprehensive, data-driven QAPI program, governance, data review, performance-improvement projects, and documentation.
  • CMS QAPI Description and Background CMS source context for quality assurance and performance improvement programs in nursing homes.
  • CMS QAPI at a Glance CMS guide to the five QAPI elements and practical tools for establishing and operating a nursing-home QAPI program.
  • QTSO Sample iQIES MDS 3.0 Reports Official migration context and sample formats for MDS Provider and Quality Measure reports moved from CASPER to iQIES in April 2023.
  • Medicare Care Compare Public CMS nursing home quality measures, Five-Star ratings, staffing information, inspection history, and facility comparisons.
