Official source orientation

  • Scope & Severity Grid CMS scoring methodology showing how scope and severity levels influence survey impact, deficiency weighting, follow-up expectations, and regulatory risk.
  • 42 CFR Part 483 The federal regulatory requirements for long-term care facilities, including resident rights, quality of care, QAPI, infection control, and administration.
  • CMS QAPI Description and Background CMS source context for quality assurance and performance improvement programs in nursing homes.
  • Medicare Care Compare Public CMS nursing home quality measures, Five-Star ratings, staffing information, inspection history, and facility comparisons.
