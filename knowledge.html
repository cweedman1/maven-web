Practice provenance

The operating experience behind the Field Manual.

Maven's educational material connects official sources with the realities of investigation, corrective action, monitoring, and accountable follow-through.

Christopher Weedman

Christopher founded Evo Engineering and designed Maven Compliance after nearly fourteen years in nursing-home administration and executive operations. As a facility administrator, he led the QAPI program with his staff and held responsibility for survey readiness, plans of correction, quality improvement, and operational follow-through. His roles also included chief operating officer and regional interim executive leadership, with multi-facility responsibility for leadership transitions and operational stabilization.

That experience informs Maven's focus on preserved evidence, assigned work, monitoring, professional review, and documented closure. Maven remains a technology platform, not an individual consulting service, and facility leaders retain responsibility for decisions and regulatory compliance.

While serving as Colonial Manor's administrator, Christopher wrote and prepared the facility's 2017 Bronze Award application. Colonial Manor in Cleburne was subsequently named a 2017 AHCA/NCAL Bronze – Commitment to Quality Award recipient. The award recognized the facility and its team.
