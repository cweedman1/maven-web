The operating model

From evidence to closure, without losing the thread.

Maven is not a document generator with a compliance theme. It is an operational structure for the work that begins when a finding, incident, or quality signal enters the facility.

Controlled workflow
01 / SOURCEPreserveKeep the survey, narrative, attachment, and source context.

04 / REVIEWDecideRecord professional review, outcome, and decision rationale.

06 / HISTORYCloseRetain what happened, why it happened, and what changed afterward.
The work behind the record

Compliance is a live operating condition.

A survey response, investigation, or QAPI priority cannot be understood from its final document alone. Maven keeps the source, people, decisions, assignments, blockers, and follow-through in view.
