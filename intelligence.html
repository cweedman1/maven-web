Facility Intelligence

Connect CASPER, QAPI, investigations, and survey history into operational priorities.

Operational Input

Maven organizes CASPER reports, QAPI summaries, investigations, POCs, survey history, and monitoring results so leadership can review facility risk in context.

System Intelligence Output

Clearer visibility into risk signals, QAPI priorities, monitoring needs, and follow-up work across operations.

What It Does

  • Aggregates investigations, POCs, CASPER reports, and facility-wide data
  • Identifies recurring risk patterns across units and time
  • Connects operational performance to QAPI review
  • Helps leadership prioritize high-impact compliance risks
  • Supports structured QAPI initiatives and monitoring steps

Why It Matters

  • Helps facilities respond to known risk before survey identifies it
  • Shows when separate events may reflect the same operational problem
  • Connects investigations, deficiencies, and CASPER trends to QAPI action
  • Improves survey readiness through clearer follow-through
  • Creates a continuous review loop across operations

Beyond QAPI

Facility intelligence is broader than a QAPI meeting. It connects CASPER review, Medicare Care Compare context, survey findings, investigations, staffing observations, audits, and monitoring results into one operational picture.
